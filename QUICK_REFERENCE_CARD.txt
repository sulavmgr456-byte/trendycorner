┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  WHEN OWNER GIVES YOU NEW PRODUCTS:                          │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

STEP 1: Upload Images
└─→ Go to: https://imgbb.com
└─→ Upload product photos
└─→ Copy the "Direct link"
└─→ Save links in a text file

STEP 2: Fill Out Template  
└─→ Open: PRODUCT_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.txt
└─→ Fill in product details
└─→ Paste image links

STEP 3: Send to AI Assistant (Me!)
└─→ Copy your filled template
└─→ Paste in chat
└─→ Say "Please add this product"

STEP 4: I'll Respond With Updated File
└─→ I'll update products.json for you
└─→ I'll tell you it's ready

STEP 5: Upload to Website
└─→ Netlify: Drag products.json file
└─→ OR cPanel: Upload via File Manager
└─→ Wait 30 seconds

STEP 6: Check Website!
└─→ Visit your shop page
└─→ See new product listed
└─→ Click on it to verify
└─→ ✅ Done!
